# 2026-05-25 — Phase 12: Overlap-Aware Embedding A/B (Negative)

## Summary

Tried an upstream attack on the 0.7935 / 0.7032 macro_f1 ceiling
diagnosed in Phase 10–11. Hypothesis: the 188-seg contaminated cluster
is dominated by overlap-corrupted embeddings; if we splice the
**RMS-dominant separated channel** back into the source PCM before
re-extracting CAM++ / WavLM-ECAPA, the fused fixture should cluster
cleaner.

Result: **embedding-side overlap patching hurts both windows.**

## Pipeline

New self-contained tool `tools/build_overlap_patched_pcm.cpp`:

1. Load `/tmp/test_mp3_16k_mono.f32` + 1169-row refined GT JSONL.
2. For each GT seg, build a 10 s zero-padded window centered on it and
   run `OverlapDetector.detect()` (pyannote-seg3 powerset, 589 frames).
3. Compute per-seg `overlap_ratio` over frames covering `[s0,s1]`. If
   `≥ 0.20` (default), route the seg PCM through
   `SpeechSeparator.separate()` (MossFormer2-SS-16k), pick
   `dom = argmax(energy_source_k)`, rescale to original RMS, splice
   `clip(dom * scale, ±1)` back into a `patched[]` PCM copy.
4. Emit `/tmp/test_mp3_16k_mono_overlap_dom.f32` (220.6 MB) and
   `tests/fixtures/overlap_patch_v1.jsonl` (per-seg overlap_ratio +
   separated flag).
5. Re-extract CAM++ and WavLM-ECAPA over the patched PCM →
   `tests/fixtures/{cam,wl}_embeddings_v2.{f32,meta.json}`.
6. Fuse with `tools/dump_fused_fixture_v2.py` (same schema as v1,
   strategy=full, fused = L2(concat(L2(cam_v), L2(wl_v)))) →
   `tests/fixtures/fused_v2_dominant.bin` (1169 × 384, 1828336 bytes).

## Dry-run histogram (overlap_ratio over 1169 GT segs)

`mean=0.0970, max=1.0`; counts `>0.05: 280, >0.10: 251, >0.20: 193,
>0.30: 146, >0.50: 88`. Real separation pass:

## A/B against Phase 10 baseline

`orator_reclusterer_eval --W 180 --S 30 --min-k 2 --max-k 6 --thr 0.55
--ema 0.20`:

| fixture | end-sec | macro_f1 | K_pred | K_mapped |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `fused_v1.bin`            | 1800 | 0.7935 | 6 | 4/4 |
| `fused_v2_dominant.bin`   | 1800 | **0.7235** | 5 | 4/4 |
| `fused_v1.bin`            | 3600 | 0.7032 | 6 | 4/4 |
| `fused_v2_dominant.bin`   | 3600 | **0.6663** | 5 | 4/4 |

Delta: **−7.0 pp s1800, −3.7 pp full_60m**. Negative on both windows.

## Interpretation

- 193 / 1169 (16.5%) of GT segs cross the 0.20 threshold, but the
  separator's "dominant" channel is not lossless: at low overlap_ratio
  (just above 0.20) the network introduces phase artifacts that perturb
  CAM++/WL-ECAPA more than the original mild interference did.
- The 188-seg contaminated cluster is therefore *not* a pure overlap
  artifact. Embedding-side rewriting is the wrong axis of attack.
- Future direction: keep v1 PCM, attack the same problem in the
  clustering pass (e.g. overlap-gated PLDA shrinkage, or per-seg
  reliability weights driven by `overlap_patch_v1.jsonl`) instead of
  destroying the original waveform.

## Phase 13 — Short-Segment Error Audit + Temporal Smoothing (Marginal)

### Hypothesis chain

Phase 12 ruled out embedding-side overlap rewriting. The new working
hypothesis: short segments carry less acoustic evidence and are the
dominant error mass. If true, two attacks are possible:

- **Plan A** — Down-weight overlap-flagged segments in the affinity matrix.
- **Plan C** — Robustify short-segment labels at decision time.

### Error audit (`tools/audit_error_segs.py`)

Joined `--dump-pred` JSONL with `overlap_patch_v1.jsonl` and the
ground-truth mapping. Error vs correct enrichment on the two scoring
windows:

| Window | dur<1.0 (err) | dur<1.0 (ok) | ov≥0.20 (err) | ov≥0.20 (ok) |
| ------ | ------------- | ------------ | ------------- | ------------ |
| s1800 (139/572 err) | **53.24 %** | 26.10 % | 15.11 % | 11.32 % |
| full_60m (343/1163 err) | **46.65 %** | 24.15 % | 20.41 % | 14.88 % |

Mean error duration is 1.33 s vs 1.93 s for correct (s1800). Short-segment
enrichment is ≈2×; overlap enrichment is only 1.3–1.4×. **Plan C is the
correct axis; Plan A signal is too weak.**

Confusion (s1800, top): gt=3→0:32, gt=0→3:27, gt=3→UNMAPPED:16,
gt=0→UNMAPPED:12 — symmetric swaps between the two largest GT
speakers, consistent with brief interjections being clustered against
their dominant neighbours.

### Zero-code knob sweep (s1800, baseline 0.7935)

All existing knobs are dead or harmful in this regime:

- `--lenw` → 0.7906 (-0.29 pp)
- `--max-globals 4` → 0.5363 (-25.72 pp; forcing K=4 collapses real speakers)
- `--merge-thr 0.85` → 0.7935 (no-op, no pairs ≥0.85)
- `--purity` → 0.7960 (+0.25 pp, but K_pred=11; spurious K explosion)
- All pairwise combinations of the above degrade or stay flat.

### Phase 13C — Temporal smoothing post-pass

Added eval-only flags (no orator runtime change):

```
--smooth-short DUR --smooth-neighbors K --smooth-min-votes MV
```

For each segment with `dur < DUR` and a committed label, scan ±K
neighbours by index. Only neighbours with `dur ≥ DUR` (reliable) and a
committed label vote. If the strict top label has count ≥ MV, a strict
majority of reliable neighbours, and differs from the current label,
override.

Algorithm sits in `tools/orator_reclusterer_eval.cpp` between the
`[eval] streamed …` log and the dense-vector build; overrides are
collected into a buffer and applied atomically so the scan window is not
contaminated.

### Sweep results (partial, s1800; remainder pruned for time after pattern
was conclusive)

| DUR | MV | K | macro_f1 | overridden |
| --- | -- | - | -------- | ---------- |
| 0.8 | 2 | **6**  | **0.7982** | 41 |
| 0.8 | 2 | 8  | 0.7950 | 40 |
| 0.8 | 2 | 12 | 0.7937 | 45 |
| 0.8 | 3 | 6  | 0.7982 | 41 |
| 1.0 | 2 | 6  | 0.7957 | 59 |
| 1.0 | 2 | 8  | 0.7842 | 65 |
| 1.0 | 2 | 12 | 0.7800 | 70 |
| 1.2 | 2 | 6  | 0.7757 | 81 |
| 1.2 | 2 | 8  | 0.7604 | 88 |

(`MV=2` and `MV=3` produce identical results at DUR≤1.0 because reliable
neighbour density rarely exceeds 4.) DUR≥1.0 floods the override set
with mid-confidence segments and tips into net-negative territory.

**Best combo: DUR=0.8 K=6 MV=2 → s1800 0.7982 (+0.47 pp).**

### Full_60m validation

```
--end-sec 3600 --smooth-short 0.8 --smooth-neighbors 6 --smooth-min-votes 2
→ macro_f1 = 0.7065  (K_pred=6, K_mapped=4/4, n_scored=1163)
```

vs baseline 0.7032 → **+0.33 pp**, 70 segments overridden.

### Verdict

Both windows improve, both gains are within noise. The hypothesis
("short segs are the error mass") is empirically confirmed by the
audit, but post-pass label propagation is **too coarse**: neighbour
labels in index-space do not always reflect a single conversational
turn, so even a strict-majority guard can only correct a handful of
flips before injecting new errors.

The right next attack on the same axis is **inside** the reclusterer:
weight the affinity matrix by per-segment reliability
`w_i = min(1, dur_i / 1.5)`, so short segments contribute less to
cluster centroids without being silenced post-hoc. That requires editing
`src/orator/orator_reclusterer.cpp` — deferred.
