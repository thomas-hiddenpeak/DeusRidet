# 2026-05-23 — Step 19c + 19d Breakthrough

## Summary

Speaker-identification subsystem hit a coverage breakthrough on the
4-speaker Mandarin replay corpus. Two orthogonal fixes landed together:

- **Step 19c — MULTI-GATE.** Before the FULL identify path, slide a CAM++
  cosine probe across `seg_fbank_buf_` (1.5 s window, 0.5 s hop). If the
  minimum adjacent-window cosine falls below
  `speaker_multi_gate_threshold` (default 0.58), the VAD is flagged
  multi-speaker and identify switches to `peek_best`: read-only, no
  exemplar admission, no auto-register, no EMA, no retro-push.
- **Step 19d — SHORT-IDENTIFY with cascade-decoupling.** Re-enable the
  short-segment band (fbank ∈ [50, 150)) using dual-encoder peek_best at
  `speaker_short_identify_threshold = 0.40`, margin = 0.05. Critically,
  SHORT-IDENTIFY hits **do not** update `prev_seg_speaker_id_` and
  therefore cannot seed INHERIT-BROADCAST. A wrong short label costs one
  decision instead of ~10.

## Result (10-min apples-to-apples replay, online_replay_score)

| Stage | coverage | decided_macro | speakers seen |
|-------|---------:|--------------:|--------------:|
| Pre-19c baseline | 0.253 | 0.854 | 3 / 4 |
| 19c MULTI-GATE only | 0.293 | 0.949 | 3 / 4 |
| **19c + 19d (shipped)** | **0.439 – 0.475** | **0.934 – 0.956** | **4 / 4** |

Net delta: coverage **+18.6 pts**, decided_macro **+9.2 pts**. All four
speakers (including 石一) recover with per-speaker decided accuracy
≥ 0.88. Threshold sweep at thr ∈ {0.55, 0.50, 0.45, 0.40, 0.35} shows
coverage rises monotonically until 0.40 then saturates; dec_macro stays
flat ≥ 0.93 throughout — landscape became smooth once the cascade was
broken.

## Diagnostic detour worth recording

A first 19d attempt zero-padded short PCM to 1 s so WL-ECAPA could run.
Coverage shot to 0.90 but decided_macro **collapsed to 0.27**: the
padded silence embedding pulled `peek_best` onto whichever centroid was
dominant, and INHERIT-BROADCAST propagated that wrong label across
~150 segments. The padding was reverted; the cascade was attacked
directly by removing the three `prev_seg_speaker_id_` writes inside
the SHORT-IDENTIFY hit branch. That single edit changed the threshold
landscape from binary to monotone.

Lesson: when a wide threshold sweep produces a binary
breakthrough-or-collapse, look for an off-axis amplification path
(here: INHERIT-BROADCAST). Tuning the threshold itself is the wrong
search dimension.

## Step 21 — SHORT-IDENTIFY → prev_full recency refresh (inconclusive, knob shipped default-OFF)

### Hypothesis

In Step 20 (4 s inherit-recency), SHORT-IDENTIFY labels apply only to
their own segment — they never seed INHERIT-BROADCAST. A strong SI hit
(sim ≥ 0.55–0.60 + ≥0.05 margin) is high-confidence enough that the
labelled identity could plausibly refresh `prev_full_time_`, extending
the 4 s inherit window across a SI hit so subsequent short backchannels
inherit the same name. Risk: a wrong SI label cascades; the margin gate
already filters absorb-into-dominant on the SI side, but adds another
amplification path.

### Implementation

`src/sensus/auditus/audio_pipeline_process_saas_full.cpp` —
inside the SHORT-IDENTIFY match branch, after `on_speaker_(match_si)`,
optionally write the matched identity into `prev_full_speaker_id_`,
`prev_full_speaker_name_`, `prev_full_time_`. Gated by config
`speaker_si_refresh_prev_full_threshold` (default 0.0 = disabled),
with env override `DEUSRIDET_SI_REFRESH_PREVFULL_THR` for sweeps.

### Sweep result (tests/test.mp3, 600 s, replay 1x, 198 GT segs)

| Run | thr | n_decided | coverage | dec_macro | per_spk_decided floor |
|-----|-----|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------------------|
| Step 20 baseline | 0.0 (off) | 113 | 0.571 | 0.921 | ≥0.90 |
| Step 21 run 1 | 0.55 | 117 | 0.591 | 0.894 | 0.815 |
| Step 21 run 2 | 0.60 | 121 | 0.611 | 0.903 | 0.875 |
| Step 21 verify | 0.60 | 110 | 0.556 | 0.899 | 0.857 |

### Verdict

Run 2 at thr=0.60 appeared a strict-Pareto win over Step 20 (+0.040
coverage, −0.018 dec_macro). The verification re-run at the same
threshold collapsed to cov=0.556 — below the Step 20 baseline. The
two thr=0.60 runs differ by 0.055 in coverage on the same fixture and
same config. **Run-to-run variance ≥ 0.055 on a 10-minute fixture
exceeds the proposed signal.** The conservative honest action is to
ship the configuration knob (so the lever exists) but default it to
0.0 (= Step 20 behaviour) until a larger fixture can discriminate
real effect from noise.

- `src/sensus/auditus/audio_pipeline_process_saas_full.cpp` — SI match

### Process lesson

A two-run protocol (any change must be confirmed on an independent
replay) was sufficient to catch this. Future micro-optimisations on
this fixture must show ≥ 2 σ over the established run variance, not
single-run point improvements.

## Step 23 — SHORT-IDENTIFY WL-ECAPA tile-padding (negative result, scaffolding shipped default-OFF)

### Diagnostic motivation

Root-cause analysis on the Step 22 thr=0.60 r1 awaken log revealed three
event populations in the SI band (fbank ∈ [50,150)):
- SI-match: 120
- SI-abstain: 68 (thresh=36, margin=32)
- **SI-skip (WL-ECAPA empty, speech_pcm_buf < 16000): 223**

The 223 SI-skip count is ~3× SI-match+abstain combined and represents
sub-1 s VADs where WL-ECAPA cannot extract an embedding (it requires
≥1 s of real PCM). These VADs miss SI entirely and only get rescued
by INHERIT-BROADCAST if prev_full is fresh (≤4 s). Separately, 108/108
no_segment GTs overlap a VAD interval — the speaker stage is the
bottleneck, not VAD.

### Hypothesis & fix

Zero-padding to 1 s was previously documented to collapse macro
(dec_macro 0.95 → 0.27) because silence dominates the ECAPA stat pool.
**Tile-padding** (looping actual speech samples up to 16000) preserves
the mean/variance/energy moments while filling temporal context. Code
gated by `speaker_si_wl_tile_pad_enable` (config + env override
`DEUSRIDET_SI_WL_TILE_PAD`). Read-only inference path (peek_best only,
no admit/EMA/exemplar).

### Ablation (1800 s reference, thr=0.60)

| floor | runs | n_decided | cov mean | cov σ | dec_macro | macro |
|------:|----:|----------:|---------:|------:|----------:|------:|
| baseline (no tile-pad) | 3 | 344 | 0.620 | 0.013 | 0.78 | 0.46 |
| 8000 (factor ≤2×) | 2 | 358 | 0.628 | **0.022** | 0.788 | 0.481 |
| 12000 (factor ≤1.33×) | 1 | 350 | 0.613 | — | 0.777 | 0.459 |

### Verdict

Negative. No floor produced a signal exceeding noise:
- 8k floor: SI events 120→150–169 (+30–49 recovered) but cov σ doubles
  to 0.022 — the periodicity artefact of low-factor repetition pollutes
  the ECAPA stat pool more than it helps.
- 12k floor: only ~25 of 223 skips recovered (most sub-1 s speech is
  < 0.75 s), macro lands within 1σ of baseline.

The 223 skip reservoir is real but requires a proper short-segment
speaker encoder (3D-Speaker ERes2Net-base / ECAPA-TDNN-small), not a
tile-pad band-aid. Knob shipped **default OFF** as scaffolding for that
future RFC; code path is exercised by env override only.

### Process lesson

Step 23 reaches the same epistemic shape as the original Step 21:
"effect within within-config noise". The difference from Step 21 is
that we now know *why* — it's a fundamental statistical property of
ECAPA stat-pooling under low-factor repetition, not a measurement
artefact. Step 21's no-go was an artefact; Step 23's is genuine. The
proper next move is a new encoder, not more tuning. No more sweeps.

## Step 24 — CAM++ shadow store for SI-skip-wl fallback (shipped, default ON)

### Diagnostic motivation (sequel to Step 22 root-cause)

The corrected no_segment attribution on Step 22 r1 (Timeline-anchored,
delta=27801.194 s) ranked the 108 no_seg GTs as:

```
46  SI-skip-wl + Timeline
26  SI-skip-wl
 9  SI-abstain-margin + Timeline
 4  Timeline-only
 4  SI-abstain-thresh + SI-skip-wl + Timeline
 3  VAD_NO_LOG_EVENT  (real sub-0.5s misses)
 3  SI-abstain-thresh
 ...
```

**82 of 108 no_segs (76 %) touched a SI-skip-wl event** — sub-1 s VADs
where WL-ECAPA cannot extract (it needs ≥ 1 s of real PCM). Only 3 were
genuine VAD misses. Step 23 already proved that synthesizing PCM
(tile/zero pad) corrupts ECAPA stat pooling. The proper fix is to keep
the dual-encoder identification path but provide a **single-encoder
fallback** for the sub-1 s band, using CAM++ alone (CAM++ has no PCM
length floor; it consumes fbank ≥ 50).

### Design

Build a parallel `SpeakerVectorStore` (`campp_db_`, 192 D) that mirrors
every confirmed `dual_db_` FULL admission of the CAM++ slice under the
**same external `speaker_id`**. SI-skip-wl events then `peek_best`
against `campp_db_` and route through the existing SI thr/margin gate
(thr=0.40, margin=0.05).

Key invariants:
- Mirror only fires when `match.speaker_id ≥ 0` (post-abstain), so the
  shadow can never get exemplars the canonical store rejected.
- Same `external_id` namespace → no downstream remapping.
- Shadow has no admit gates of its own (accepts every CAM++ exemplar
  the canonical path admitted). Acceptable for 1800 s; for longer runs
  the shadow may need its own EMA pruning. Documented but not built.
- Read-only on the SI-skip-wl path (no admit / no EMA / no exemplar
  bump). The shadow's exemplar count grows only via FULL mirror.

### Implementation surface

- `src/orator/speaker_vector_store.{h,cu}` — new
  `register_speaker_with_id(int id, const std::vector<float>& emb)`
  that creates a slot with a caller-chosen external id (rejects
  collisions, bumps `next_id_`).
- `src/sensus/auditus/audio_pipeline.h` — new bool
  `speaker_campp_shadow_enable` (default `true`).
- `src/sensus/auditus/audio_pipeline_process_saas_full.cpp` — two
  insertions:
  1. **FULL mirror** (after recency-absorb + margin-abstain gates):
     `register_speaker_with_id` for `match.is_new`, `add_exemplar`
     for incremental case.
  2. **SI-skip-wl fallback** (inside the dual-encoder block): if
     WL-ECAPA empty AND shadow has speakers, peek_best on CAM++ slice,
     then apply same SI gates and emit `SHORT-IDENTIFY campp-shadow`
     log + identify event.
- `src/actus/awaken.cpp` — config load + banner field
  `campp_shadow=ON/off`.
- `configs/auditus.conf` — bilingual doc + knob.

### Results (1800 s fixture, SI ON thr=0.40 margin=0.05 min_fb=50, MULTI-GATE thr=0.58 min_fb=250)

| Run | n_dec | n_abs | n_no_seg | cov | macro | dec_macro |
|-----|-------|-------|----------|-----|-------|-----------|
| Step 22 r1 (baseline) | 354 | ~108 | 108 | 0.620 | 0.460 | 0.780 |
| Step 24 r1            | 382 |  118 |  71 | 0.669 | 0.483 | 0.753 |
| Step 24 r2            | 384 |  114 |  73 | 0.673 | 0.473 | 0.754 |
| **Step 24 mean**      | **383** | **116** | **72** | **0.671** | **0.478** | **0.754** |
| **Δ vs baseline**     | **+29** | **+8** | **−36** | **+0.051 (≈3.9σ)** | **+0.018 (≈1.4σ)** | **−0.026** |

`σ` on coverage is 0.013 from Step 22's six 1800 s runs. The +0.051
coverage shift is 3.9× that noise — unambiguous. Macro improves by
~1.4σ; decided-macro drops by ~2σ because the rescued sub-1 s segments
are intrinsically noisier than the dual-encoder full-pool segments
(76 % accuracy vs 89 %). Net macro is still positive — every recovered
sub-1 s correct call outweighs its mate's wrong one.

Per-speaker accuracy preserves the relative ranking
(石一 > 朱杰 > 唐云峰 ≈ 徐子景) and the all-speakers-decided coverage
gain holds for every speaker, not just one.

### Verdict

Ship default ON. `campp_shadow=ON` in banner. SI-skip-wl reservoir
shrinks 223 → ~50 (about 173 events recovered per 1800 s run; 47 were
visible in the first 5 min of r1's `SHORT-IDENTIFY campp-shadow` log
lines). The remaining ~50 SI-skip-wl events are either pre-FULL (no
shadow exemplar yet) or fail the SI thr=0.40 gate. The 3 genuine
VAD-miss cases remain (sub-0.5 s speech) — those are a separate VAD
sensitivity problem, not a speaker-ID problem.

### Follow-ups (not blocking ship)

- Shadow store has no admit gates. For multi-hour runs, the canonical
  store's margin-abstain protects `dual_db_` from drift; the shadow
  inherits whatever `dual_db_` admitted. If `dual_db_` ever admits a
  borderline FULL, the shadow gets the same exemplar with no second
  vote. Mitigation: gate mirror on `match.similarity ≥ campp_admit_thr`.
- For runs > 1 h the shadow may need its own EMA / max-exemplar cap.
- The proper long-term fix is still a dedicated short-segment encoder
  (ERes2Net-small / ECAPA-small) — Step 24 unblocks short-segment
  coverage without that dependency.

## Step 24-b — calibrate CAM++ shadow gate (shipped, default 0.48/0.10)

### Why a recalibration was needed

Step 24 (shadow store, gates inherited from SI: thr=0.40 margin=0.05)
moved the 1800 s fixture from baseline cov=0.620 macro=0.460
dec_macro=0.780 to cov=0.671 macro=0.478 **dec_macro=0.754**. The
coverage gain was real (+3.9σ on cov), but **dec_macro regressed by
−0.026** — the rescued segments were noisier than the existing
decision pool. Net macro effect was only +1.4σ.

Distribution of `SHORT-IDENTIFY campp-shadow` similarity (r1, n=222):

```
min=0.169  p25=0.382  p50=0.452  p75=0.524  p90=0.567  max=0.706
mean=0.440
```

At thr=0.40, **146/222 events passed** with sim-mean barely above the
gate. Arithmetic: 29 new decisions over baseline at dec_macro 0.754 vs
0.780 implies the new shadow admits were only ~45 % accurate (vs
baseline ~78 %). **Root cause: the SI gate (thr=0.40 m=0.05) was tuned
for the dual-encoder 384-D vector. Single-encoder 192-D CAM++ has
weaker discrimination — reusing the same gate is the bug.**

### Design

Promote shadow gates to first-class config:

```cpp
// audio_pipeline.h
float speaker_campp_shadow_threshold = 0.55f;  // default; tuned below
float speaker_campp_shadow_margin    = 0.10f;
```

Threaded a `bool peek_from_shadow` flag through the SI-skip-wl branch.
At the downstream gate, when set, the code substitutes
`(eff_thresh, eff_margin)` for `(si_thresh, si_margin)`. New abstain
log lines:

```
SHORT-IDENTIFY abstain (shadow-thresh): ...
SHORT-IDENTIFY abstain (margin-shadow): ...
```

Same `awaken.cpp` config loader + banner extension. The regular SI gate
is unchanged for the dual-fused path.

### Calibration sweep (1800 s, σ_cov ≈ 0.013, σ_macro est. 0.010)

| run | thr | margin | cov | macro | dec_macro | n_no_seg |
|-----|-----|--------|-----|-------|-----------|----------|
| Step 22 baseline | (no shadow) | — | 0.620 | 0.460 | 0.780 | 108 |
| Step 24 r1+r2 mean | 0.40 | 0.05 | 0.671 | 0.478 | **0.754** | 72 |
| Step 24-b r1 (thr=0.55) | 0.55 | 0.10 | 0.618 | 0.456 | 0.791 | 107 |
| Step 24-b r2 (thr=0.48) | 0.48 | 0.10 | 0.669 | **0.493** | 0.778 | 87 |
| Step 24-b r3 (thr=0.48) | 0.48 | 0.10 | 0.631 | 0.480 | 0.797 | 101 |
| **Step 24-b r2+r3 mean** | **0.48** | **0.10** | **0.650** | **0.487** | **0.788** | **94** |

- thr=0.55 is **too tight**: only 35 of 222 events pass — reverts to
  baseline (coverage gain lost, dec_macro recovered to 0.791).
- thr=0.48 is the sweet spot: 92 events pass (minus ~12 margin
  abstains), recovering ~80 of the 222 shadow events.

### Result (vs both baseline and Step 24)

| Δ | vs Step 22 baseline | vs Step 24 (0.40/0.05) |
|---|---------------------|------------------------|
| coverage | +0.030 | −0.021 |
| macro    | **+0.027 (~2.7σ)** | **+0.009** |
| dec_macro | +0.008 (recovered) | **+0.034 (regression healed)** |
| n_no_seg | −14 | +22 |

Step 24-b is **strictly better than Step 24**: macro nudges up, the
dec_macro regression is fully erased, and the small coverage trade-off
is the correct price for keeping decision quality at baseline.

### Lesson

When you reuse a gate threshold across encoders of different
dimensionality / different training objectives, **always recalibrate**.
Cosine similarity distributions are not portable: CAM++ 192-D and
dual-fused 384-D produce different absolute scales for the same
semantic distinctions. Step 24 net-positive macro masked this — the
diagnostic that revealed the true root cause was the per-new-decision
accuracy implied by dec_macro arithmetic.

### Knobs

- `speaker_campp_shadow_threshold` (default `0.48`, was `0.55` in
  initial RFC draft) — set ≤ 0 to disable the threshold tightening
  (falls back to the SI threshold).
- `speaker_campp_shadow_margin` (default `0.10`) — same disable
  convention.

## Step 25 — SI-peek veto / rescue on INHERIT-BROADCAST (NEGATIVE result)

the 24-b r2 paired decisions (n=382) by event source vs correctness:

| source              | OK | WR | err%  |
| ------------------- | -: | -: | ----: |
| fresh CAM++ ≥ 0.55  |129 | 12 |  8.5 %|
| fresh CAM++ < 0.55  | 68 |  4 |  5.6 %|
| INHERIT (sim=0)     | 39 | 14 | 26.4 %|
| no_evt (≥1 s)       | 26 | 39 | 60.0 %|
| no_evt (<1 s)       | 18 | 12 | 40.0 %|
| amend / new         | 20 |  1 |  4.8 %|

The hypothesis: when SHORT-IDENTIFY ran and abstained, its `peek_best`
result — discarded today — often carries a *correct* opinion that
disagrees with the inherit identity. Use that signal to either suppress
the broadcast (veto) or replace the label (rescue).

### Step 25a — SI-peek VETO

When SI abstained, if peek's similarity ≥ `inherit_peek_veto_threshold`
(default 0.45) and `peek.speaker_id ≠ inh_id`, drop the inherit
broadcast (emit `no_segment`) instead of forwarding a likely-wrong
label.

| run            | cov   | macro | dec_macro | vetoes |
| -------------- | ----: | ----: | --------: | -----: |
| 24-b r2 (base) | 0.650 | 0.487 |     0.788 |    0   |
| 25a r1         | 0.648 | 0.494 |     0.800 |    9   |
| 25a r2         | 0.648 | 0.483 |     0.785 |    8   |
| 25a r3 (final) | 0.639 | 0.467 |     0.785 |    -   |
| **25a mean**   | **0.645** | **0.481** | **0.790** |        |

3-run mean macro **0.481 < 0.487 baseline** — within σ on every metric
but slightly below baseline. Veto fires only ~8 times per 1800 s, too
rarely to dominate. By construction the veto converts
wrong-broadcast → `no_segment`, both scoring zero in `macro` (all-GT)
— only `dec_macro` (decided-only) can move. The expected lift on
`dec_macro` (+0.005 mean) is within run-to-run noise.

### Step 25b — SI-peek RESCUE

Stronger variant: when the veto would fire, substitute peek's identity
as the broadcast label (turning wrong inherits into correct decisions).

| run            | cov   | macro | dec_macro |
| -------------- | ----: | ----: | --------: |
| 25b r1         | 0.553 | 0.459 |     0.848 |
| 25b r2         | 0.657 | 0.485 |     0.780 |
| **25b mean**   | **0.605** | **0.472** | **0.814** |

Run-to-run variance exploded — `cov` swung 10.4 pts, `dec_macro` 6.8
pts between two replays with identical knobs. Mean `macro` 0.472
**regresses** vs 24-b 0.487. The 7 rescue events in r1 coincided with
20 fewer `INHERIT-BROADCAST` emissions and 31 fewer SI matches than
25a r1 (control), suggesting a cascading state interaction we did not
fully isolate. Disqualifying for shipping.

### Decision

- **Both knobs default off** (`speaker_inherit_peek_veto_enable = 0`,
  `speaker_inherit_peek_rescue_enable = 0`). Configs/auditus.conf
  defaults match.
- Code and config entries retained as opt-in diagnostic levers.
- The bar (macro mean ≥ 0.497 over 2 runs) was **not cleared** by any
  Step 25 variant. The intervention surface — `INHERIT-BROADCAST` and
  SI peek — is too narrow to move the macro denominator at scale,
  because:
  1. veto moves only `dec_macro`, by construction;
  2. rescue is unstable (state cascade through `prev_*` chain);
  3. the dominant error class is `no_evt` (75 % of wrongs), which is a
     **scorer-pairing** artefact (event-vs-VAD time mismatch), not a
     model error this layer can fix.
- The honest next direction is to attack `no_evt` directly: either
  in the pairing logic (`online_replay_score.py`) or by making the
  runtime emit a "decision event" closer to the VAD mid-time rather
  than relying on SAAS-segend timing. That is out of Step 25 scope.

## Phase 0 + Phase 1 — Architecture ceiling probe and streaming-reclusterer PoC

After Step 25's negative result, stopped tuning the online state machine
and measured the **architecture ceiling** on the existing GT-aligned
embedding dump (`tests/fixtures/{cam,wl}_embeddings_v1.{f32,meta.json}`,
1169 GT segments × 5 window strategies × 192D).

### Phase 0 — encoders are fine

`tools/offline_ceiling.py` runs three offline clusterers (AHC, Spectral,
AHC-NME) on three encoder choices (CAM++, WL-ECAPA, fused 384D) across
four subsets:

| Configuration (s1800, 571 segs)             | macro |
|---------------------------------------------|------:|
| Current runtime (online cosine state mach.) | 0.487 |
| Oracle **AHC** K=4 (≈ runtime `identify()`) | 0.166 |
| **Oracle Spectral K=4 (fused/full)**        | **0.773** |
| Oracle Spectral K=4, long ≥1.5s only        | **0.947** |

**Diagnosis:** the encoders carry more than enough information. The
AHC/centroid-cosine primitive used by `SpeakerVectorStore::identify()`
has a hard ceiling at macro ≈ 0.17 on this data — that is *the* reason
every Step 19–25 patch kept fluctuating near 0.49. The matcher primitive
is the bottleneck, not the encoder, not the gates.

### Phase 1 PoC — streaming spectral re-cluster

`tools/phase1_simulate.py` simulates a background re-clusterer:
rolling window of W seconds, fires every S seconds, spectral clustering
on cosine affinity, persistent global IDs via Hungarian centroid matching
with a link threshold.

Best config (W=600s, S=30s, link_thr=0.55, oracle K=4):

| Fixture          | n_segs | macro | speakers tracked |
|------------------|-------:|------:|-----------------:|
| s1800 (30 min)   |    571 | **0.764** |    6 |
| full_60m         |   1162 | **0.681** |    6 |

Auto-K via fixed eigengap (skip the first connected-component gap):
macro 0.672 on s1800. No K oracle needed in production.

**Headline delta vs current runtime:** +0.28 macro on 30-min, +0.19 on
60-min. Real-world runtime degradation expected ≈ 0.05 from PoC due to
VAD-split mismatch and short-segment encoder failures, so a conservative
end-to-end expectation is **macro 0.65–0.72**.

### Decision

- Encoders: keep as is.
- All online INHERIT-BROADCAST / SI-peek / shadow-gate logic: keep for
  low-latency tentative labels, but they will be **superseded** by the
  re-clusterer's retroactive commits.
- Next step: implement C++ `OratorReclusterer` (window buffer + spectral
  + Hungarian linker + WebSocket `speaker_relabel` events).

### Files

- `tools/offline_ceiling.py` — encoder × clusterer × subset ceiling matrix.
- `tools/phase1_simulate.py` — streaming spectral PoC with W/S/K/thr knobs.

## Phase 3 — global-centroid merge (negative finding)

Attempted to close the `K_pred=5` vs `K_gt=4` gap by collapsing
near-duplicate global centroids after each spectral pass.

**Implementation.** New config knobs
`global_merge_threshold` (cos sim above which two globals collapse) and
`global_merge_support_ratio` (gate: merge only if `min_support ≤ ratio ·
max_support`, default 0.5). Merge picks `keep_id` = the global with
larger `support_count`, weighted-averages centroids, L2-normalises,
remaps `local_to_global[]` and previously-committed buffer slots, and
walks `committed_history_` to emit retroactive `RelabelEvent`s for
segments already evicted from the window. Disabled by default
(`global_merge_threshold = -1.0`).

**Diagnostic (s1800, K=4 oracle, link_thr=0.50, ema=0.20).** Per-global
GT histogram exposed the failure mode:

| pred  | total | hist [gt0,gt1,gt2,gt3] | mapped |
|-------|------:|------------------------|-------:|
| 1000  | 146   | [132, 2, 1, 11]         | gt0    |
| 1001  | 18    | [1, 1, 0, 16]           | unmapped (fragment of gt3) |
| 1002  | 149   | [2, 0, 1, 146]          | gt3    |
| 1003  | 188   | [44, 16, 60, 68]        | gt2 (heavily mixed) |
| 1004  | 71    | [3, 57, 4, 7]           | gt1    |

Pairwise centroid cosines (final state):

| pair         | cos    | supports |
|--------------|-------:|----------|
| (1001, 1002) | 0.8727 | 702 / 2477 |
| (1001, 1003) | 0.8455 | 702 / 2378 |
| (1002, 1003) | 0.7224 | 2477 / 2378 |

The correct merge is `1001 → 1002` (both gt3). But cosine cannot
distinguish it from `1001 → 1003`, because the dirty cluster 1003
contains 68 gt3 samples that pull its centroid into the gt3 region.

**Sweep results (s1800, K=4 oracle, ema=0.20).**

| merge_thr | macro_f1 | K_pred | note                                  |
|-----------|---------:|-------:|---------------------------------------|
| disabled  | 0.6971   |   5    | baseline                              |
| 0.85      | 0.5399   |   4    | no disparity gate; merged wrong pair  |
| 0.80      | 0.5421   |   4    | no disparity gate; merged wrong pair  |
| 0.80      | 0.6761   |   4    | with disparity gate ≤ 0.5              |
| 0.85      | 0.6761   |   4    | with disparity gate ≤ 0.5              |

Even with the support-disparity gate (`min_support ≤ 0.5 · max_support`,
which excludes the equally-large 1002/1003 pair), the merge still chose
the wrong direction in at least one streaming tick and degraded macro
by 2.1 pp.

**Conclusion.** Pairwise cosine-based centroid merging is fundamentally
unreliable when one of the fragments is a topic-mixed "junk" cluster
whose centroid sits between two real classes. The infrastructure is
shipped (`global_merge_threshold`, disparity gate, retroactive
relabeling via `committed_history_`) but defaults to disabled. The
Phase-2 result `macro=0.6971` on s1800 / `0.7126` on full_60m stands —
both exceed the 0.65 acceptance gate. Closing the K_pred gap will
require a different signal (e.g. a centroid-quality metric, or
discriminative re-clustering of the mixed cluster's segments rather
than a centroid merge).
